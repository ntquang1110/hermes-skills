# Pig Disease Data Vietnam — Condensed (research 14/09/2026)

## Market size
- Total pig herd: 31.08 million (end 2024, including 4.48M unweaned piglets)
- Marketed pigs/year: ~80-100 million weaned piglets (extrapolated)
- Animal health market VN: 194.95M USD (2024) → 248.82M USD (2033), CAGR 2.47%
- Vet drug spend: ~1.5-1.7M VND/pig/litter (vaccine + antibiotics + supplements)

## Disease prevalence (pig)

### Most common (year-round, "cơm bữa")
| Disease | Prevalence | Source |
|---------|-----------|--------|
| PWD/ETEC (scours) | E. coli 96.7% samples, C. perfringens 75.6% | Mindfarm 2024-2025, 131 samples, 3 provinces |
| PRDC (respiratory complex) | 53-58% post-weaning, 42-47% finishing | Ruggeri 2020 (Virbac) |
| Roundworm | 39.2% (<2mo), 48% (3-4mo) | Bamboovet |
| Mycoplasma suis | Common, subclinical | Bamboovet |

### Most costly (economic damage)
| Disease | Damage | Source |
|---------|--------|--------|
| ASF (African Swine Fever) | 3,600B VND (2019 alone, 52 provinces, 2.2M culled). Total ~6M culled since 2019. | VnExpress 2019, VNUA 2026 |
| PRRS (Blue Ear) | 1.5x damage when co-infected with M. hyo | Acevet |
| PRDC | 12-15% ADG reduction; ~2.8 USD/pig (AU) | Virbac/Wyburn 2015 |

### Regulatory
- **NĐ 211/2026/ND-CP**: bans antibiotic growth promoters (AGP) in feed from 01/01/2026
- EU banned ZnO high-dose 2022 → VN following
- ASF vaccine: NAVET-ASFVAC 34-36k/dose, ~6M doses by 6/2024; AVAC ASF LIVE; DACOVAC
- ASF 2024-2026: endemic circulation, new recombinant genotype I+II (North: 60% genotype II, 40% recombinant)
- 70-80% ASF transmission via humans
