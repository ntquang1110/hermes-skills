## 2. High pH (12-14) Mechanism

### CRITICAL CORRECTION (from PubMed research 25/09/2026):
- **pH 13-14 ALONE at ambient temp is NOT sufficient for non-enveloped viruses.** PMID 21333548: non-enveloped viruses (PCV2, PPV, SVDV) need pH 9.5 + 60C for 10h. PMID 9192351: NaOH needs QAC (DDAC) to kill SVDV in 1 min — NaOH alone ineffective.
- **NaOH fails in organic matter.** PMID 4631432: 2% NaOH (pH >13) FAILED against ASFV in blood-spleen homogenate at 22-25C in 1h.
- **FMDV is alkali-stable.** It's acid-labile, not alkali-sensitive. Alkaline disinfection is weaker vs FMDV — use acid or oxidizers.
- **Lime durability collapses at 3 days** in litter (PMID 28321028). Must re-apply 2x/week.

### Kill mechanism by pathogen group:

| Pathogen group | Killed at pH | Contact time | Limitation |
|----------------|--------------|-------------|------------|
| Enveloped viruses (ASFV, PRRSV, CSFV, PRV, SIV, PEDV, TGEV) | pH>11 | 1-5 min | Organic matter interferes |
| Non-enveloped viruses (PCV2, PPV, SVDV) | pH>12.5 + QAC or heat | 30-60 min | pH alone at ambient = NOT reliable |
| FMDV (non-enveloped but acid-labile) | pH<6 (acid) > pH>9 (alkali) | 5-10 min | Use acid citric 0.2% or oxidizer |
| Gram- bacteria (E. coli, Salmonella, Pasteurella, etc.) | pH>10-11 | 5-15 min | Organic matter buffers pH |
| Gram+ bacteria (Strep suis, Staph hyicus) | pH>11-12 | 10-20 min | Thick PG resists longer |
| Mycoplasma (no cell wall) | pH>10 | 1-5 min | Easiest to kill |
| Clostridium spores | pH>12.5 + 60C | 30-60 min | Most resistant. Need heat + chemical combo |

## 4. The Intersection — Common Kill Mechanism Analysis

### Can one product kill ALL swine pathogens?

**NO single mechanism kills everything.** Here is the real intersection:

| Mechanism | Kills | Misses |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| High pH (13-14) alone | Enveloped viruses, Gram- bacteria, Gram+ bacteria, Mycoplasma | Non-enveloped viruses (PCV2, PPV), FMDV (alkali-stable), Clostridium spores, organic matter |
| Oxidizers (chlorine, peroxygen) | Everything including spores | Expensive, quenched by organic matter, chlorine loses potency at high pH |
| Aldehydes (glutaraldehyde) | Everything including spores | Toxic, slow, needs clean surfaces |
| QAC (benzalkonium chloride) | Enveloped viruses, Gram+ bacteria | Non-enveloped viruses, Gram- (variable) |

### The product formula that covers ALL:

**Base: NaOH/KOH — pH 13-14** → kills enveloped viruses + Gram- bacteria + Gram+ bacteria + Mycoplasma

**+ QAC (benzalkonium chloride 5-10%)** → kills non-enveloped viruses (PCV2, PPV) via QAC micelle aggregation of damaged particles (PMID 9192351: DDAC + 0.1% NaOH effective vs SVDV in 1 min)

**+ Mild oxidizer (H2O2 1-3%)** → handles organic matter interference + kills spores

**+ Pre-clean requirement** → remove organic matter before applying

**Estimated cost: ~100,000-150,000 VND/L concentrate** — cheaper than Bencocid (85,000/L but weaker spectrum) and Virkon (250,000/kg).

### Practical farm protocol (proven):
1. Remove manure, pressure wash
2. Apply NaOH 2-3% + QAC 0.5-1%, 30 min contact
3. Rinse
4. For high-risk: add H2O2 1% to step 2
5. Dry 24h
6. Re-apply lime on floors 2x/week
