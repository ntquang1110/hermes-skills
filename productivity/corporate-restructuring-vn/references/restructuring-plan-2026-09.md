# Restructuring Plan — Mana Đất Vàng (September 2026)

## Context

Founder (Quang) meeting with partner (Quân, cousin/nephew — gọi mẹ bằng cô) to restructure family business. Quân is currently legal representative of Mana Đất Vàng.

## Current Structure

```
Mana Đất Vàng (existing legal entity)
├── Mẹ: Chủ tịch HĐQT (currently also involved in daily operations)
├── Quân: Đại diện pháp luật (legal representative)
└── Quang: Phó Giám đốc
```

- 5 tỷ VND in company bank account
- EZ80 is the main product, owned by Mana Đất Vàng
- Just completed a capital raise round
- No accountant yet
- Multiple product claims for EZ80 (too many — needs focus)

## Target Structure

```
Mana Đất Vàng (same legal entity)
├── Mẹ: Chủ tịch HĐQT — chiến lược, không điều hành
├── Quân: Đại diện PL + Kinh doanh + Kỹ thuật
└── Quang: Phó Giám đốc — Sản phẩm + R&D
```

- Flat structure: both report directly to mother
- Decision threshold: spending over X triệu (proposed 50-100tr) needs mother
- Quang needs signing authorization from Quân for his domain
- 1-page role charter defining boundaries

## Persuasion Strategy for Mother

**Goal:** Move mother from daily operations to strategic governance role.

**Key framing:** "Con muốn mẹ lên Chủ tịch HĐQT, không phải đuổi mẹ ra. Công ty lớn thì phải có HĐQT, có ban điều hành. Mẹ chốt chiến lược, tụi con chạy."

**Timeline:**
- Week 1-2: Silent preparation (hire accountant, get office, show results)
- End of week 2: Formal proposal with new role definition
- Month 1-2: Prove with results + maintain weekly connection

**Pitfalls to avoid:**
- Never let Quân confront mother directly — Quang is the bridge
- Never say "tách mẹ ra" — say "nâng mẹ lên"
- Don't rush — mother needs to see results before trusting

## Financial Situation

- 5 tỷ VND in Mana Đất Vàng account
- Need allocation: R&D budget, office/lab setup, inventory, contingency
- Need accountant first (15-20tr/month range)
- Need to present to shareholders with clear allocation plan

## Product Roadmap

- EZ80: focus on 1-2 hero claims (too many currently), repackage
- 2 new formulas: base Kiềm Thảo Dược + new active ingredients
- MVP timeline: 4-6 weeks per formula
- R&D lab setup needed

## Key Insight

With 5 tỷ capital and ~1.2 tỷ budget for 6 months, the company has significant runway. However, this doesn't account for founder salaries (Quang and Quân didn't take salaries) and assumes EZ80 revenue covers production costs. The remaining 3.56 tỷ after 6 months is available for scaling or as war chest.

## Key Decisions Made

1. Keep Mana Đất Vàng as the single legal entity (no new company)
2. EZ80 stays with Mana Đất Vàng
3. Quân = legal rep + sales + technical
4. Quang = deputy director + product + R&D
5. Mother = Chairwoman, strategic only
6. Flat reporting structure to mother
7. Quân is cousin (cháu gọi mẹ bằng cô) — family member, not outsider
