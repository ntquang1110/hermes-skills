## Each Module Structure

Every module has 4 sections:

```
1. 📖 Theory — 2-3 frameworks with explanation + visual (table/framework-box)
2. 🎯 Case Study — 1 real leader example (Nadella, Jobs, Ferguson, etc.)
3. ✍️ Reflection — 3-5 questions to apply to user's context
4. ✅ Action Tasks — 3 concrete tasks to do before next module
```

## Reflection Questions Template (Module 1)

1. **Trigger map:** What makes you lose composure with your team? Why?
2. **Values audit:** Top 3 values you want your team to feel when working with you
3. **Leadership gap:** Which leadership skill are you most/least confident in?
4. **EQ check:** In the past month, when did you react vs respond?
5. **Best people:** What do the best people you've worked with have in common?

## Re-ordering Modules

After reflection, re-prioritize modules based on user's gaps. Common priority orders:

| User Signal | Priority Modules |
|-------------|-----------------|
| "My team isn't strong enough" | 4 (People), then 7 (Difficult Conversations), 5 (Teams) |
| "I'm not influential" | 3 (Communication), 2 (Trust), 6 (Vision) |
| "I don't know my direction" | 6 (Vision), 1 (Self-Mastery), 10 (Legacy) |
| "I can't keep good people" | 4 (Talent), 2 (Trust), 9 (Culture) |
