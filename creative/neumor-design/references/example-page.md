A complete working example was built for Kiềm Chăn Nuôi Saphia research briefs.

**Preview:** https://preview.thefirstonly.com/h3ixicslvbx8

**Local file:** `C:\Users\NTQUANG_DELL\hermes-temp\research-tong-hop.html`

## Structure

- Hero with decorative nested circles (extruded → inset deep → extruded)
- 7-tab navigation (inset tab bar, extruded active tabs)
- Overview panel with inset table wells, callouts, nested depth flow
- 4 product brief panels (extruded cards + prod cards with 32px radius)
- Economics panel with card grid + TAM table
- Mechanism panel with inset step flow + nested depth chain
- Inset footer
