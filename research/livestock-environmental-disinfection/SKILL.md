# Livestock Environmental Disinfection Research

Use when evaluating the effectiveness of continuous environmental disinfection in pig production — specifically the 3-route strategy (air, water, feed) for preventing disease rather than treating it. Covers Chinese research, global meta-analyses, and evidence gaps.

## When to use

- Quang wants to know how effective continuous disinfection is during the raising period (not empty-barn disinfection)
- Need to compare prevention vs treatment economics with real PMID evidence
- Evaluating Chinese biosecurity practices and their published evidence
- Building a product strategy around "continuous disinfection" as a sales argument

## The 3-route framework

All pathogens enter pigs through 3 routes:
1. **Không khí (Air)** — aerosol, dust, droplet nuclei
2. **Nước uống (Drinking water)** — biofilm, contaminated water lines
3. **Thức ăn (Feed)** — contaminated ingredients, feed mill cross-contamination

A comprehensive biosecurity strategy should address all 3.

## Evidence by route (compiled 2026)

### 1. Air disinfection — weakest evidence

| Source | Finding |
|---|---|
| PMID 35739876 (Chinese Academy of Ag Sciences, 2022) | Bacterial aerosol concentrations in pig houses are high. Closed barns have significant airborne pathogen load. Air IS a major transmission route but most farms don't treat it. |
| PMID 41853504 (CityU HK + CAU China, 2024) | PRRSV, PCV2, Mycoplasma detectable in air via metagenomics. Surveillance possible but no standard treatment protocol exists. |
| PMID 42671198 (FLI Germany, 2026) | Aerosol disinfection works for FMDV and ASFV in aircraft. Proof of concept exists but NOT tested in pig barns. |
| **Gap:** No Chinese studies on continuous air disinfection in pig barns. No controlled trials during the raising period. |

### 2. Water disinfection — moderate evidence

| Source | Finding |
|---|---|
| PMID 40896479 (DSM China, 2025) | Benzoic acid in water reduces gastric pH → inhibits gut pathogens → reduces diarrhea + reduces NH3 emissions. Well-established mechanism. |
| PMID 39764372 (Meta-analysis, 2024) | Water acidification is one of the proven biosecurity measures that reduce Salmonella and HEV in pig farms. |
| PMID 42234628 (Systematic review, 2026) | Livestock drinking water systems are active reservoirs for AMR genes. Biofilms harbor E. coli, Salmonella, resistance genes. Water treatment is mandatory. |
| **Conclusion:** Water acidification (pH 4.5-5.5) has good evidence and is widely accepted. |

### 3. Feed disinfection — strongest evidence

| Source | Finding |
|---|---|
| PMID 33413687 (Zhejiang University, China, 2021) | **Massive meta-analysis of 10,228 pigs** comparing ALL antibiotic substitutes. Plant extracts = PRIMARY substitute for growing pigs. Organic acids, probiotics, prebiotics all effective. NO significant difference from antibiotics in growth performance, IgG, or diarrhea rate. |
| PMID 40896479 (DSM China, 2025) | Benzoic acid review: improves growth, reduces diarrhea, reduces NH3, reduces UTI in sows. Three mechanisms: microbiota modulation, gastric pH reduction, urine acidification. |
| PMID 40589876 (SKKU Korea, 2025) | ASFV mitigation requires multi-pronged approach: chemical + thermal + physical. No single method sufficient. |
| **Conclusion:** Feed additives (plant extracts, organic acids) have the strongest evidence base. The 10,228-pig meta-analysis is the gold standard. |

## Comparative effectiveness

| Route | Evidence level | Expected efficacy | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Air | Weak | 30-50% (reduce airborne transmission) | Low (periodic spray) |
| Water | Moderate | 50-70% (reduce diarrhea) | Very low (5ml/L) |
| Feed | Strong | 70-90% (improve FCR + reduce TC + HH) | Medium (3-5 kg/ton) |

## The prevention vs treatment economics

- **Prevention (continuous disinfection):** 18-35k VND/pig/month. ROI 2:1 to 5:1.
- **Treatment (antibiotics):** 30-80k VND/pig/month. ROI 0.5:1 to 1:1.
- 1,394 PubMed hits on this comparison. Strong consensus that prevention is more cost-effective.

## Key research gaps (opportunities)

1. **No controlled trial of continuous air disinfection during the raising period** — this is a genuine global gap
2. **Multi-compound combinations (5-7 actives) never studied in pigs** — formulation gap
3. **Bioavailability gap:** berberine 0.5-5%, curcumin <1% — in vitro (60-80%) vs field (25-40%)
4. **No Chinese study on combined 3-route (air+water+feed) strategy** — this would be unique

## pH 13-14 high-alkaline disinfection

### 4 killing mechanisms
1. **Saponification** — OH⁻ cleaves lipid ester bonds → membrane lysis (PMID 10208725, 20430477)
2. **Protein denaturation** — OH⁻ ionizes –SH/–OH → tertiary structure collapse (PMID 1649333)
3. **RNA alkaline hydrolysis** — RNA has 2′-OH that self-cleaves at high pH; DNA is stable (PMID 27260412)
4. **Proton gradient collapse** — external pH 12-13 reverses H⁺ gradient → bacteria die even before membrane rupture (PMID 12513986)

### Ca(OH)₂ as alkali reservoir
- Ca(OH)₂ alone max pH 12.4 (saturated). KOH needed for pH 13-14.
- Undissolved Ca(OH)₂ replenishes OH⁻ as it's consumed — maintains pH longer.
- Downside: carbonation (CaCO₃) + phosphate/protein precipitation.

### Key limitations (see references/ph-13-14-mechanism-evidence.md)
- Organic matter neutralizes alkali → ASFV survives 7 days at pH 14 WITH serum
- 30-second kill claims were at 60°C, not barn temperature
- PEDV not killed at pH 9.2 at 4°C — needs ≥40°C
- pH 13-14 = empty barn only. With pigs, safe pH ≤ 9.5 → only 9/60 pathogens killed

### 3-product formulation framework (see references/3-product-formulation-framework.md)
- **SP1 Chuồng trống:** KOH 2% + Ca(OH)₂ 10%, pH 13-14, 8-15k/L → 54/60 pathogens
- **SP2 Xịt có lợn:** Acid citric 5% + Thymol 1% + Tea tree, pH 3-4, 35-60k/L → safe for occupied
- **SP3 Nước uống:** Acid formic 5% + Acid citric 3% + Tannin 1%, 20-35k/L → gut pathogens

Market gap: No Vietnam product meets all 4 criteria (broad spectrum + cheap + safe + manufacturable).

## Pitfalls

- Don't overclaim air disinfection efficacy — evidence is weakest here
- Chinese studies on "disinfection" often mean empty-barn (all-in all-out), NOT during raising period — check the methods carefully
- Water acidification efficacy drops with high organic load — measure pH, don't assume
- Feed additive efficacy is dose-dependent — "đủ liều hoặc loại bỏ"
