## Honest gaps

1. **No controlled trial of continuous air disinfection during the raising period** exists anywhere in the world. This is a genuine research gap.
2. **Chinese "disinfection" studies usually mean empty-barn (all-in all-out).** Read methods carefully before citing.
3. **Most feed additive studies are short-term (21-42 days).** Long-term effects through full fattening cycle (120 days) unknown.
4. **Multi-compound synergy never studied in pigs.** Combining 5-7 active compounds is a black box.
