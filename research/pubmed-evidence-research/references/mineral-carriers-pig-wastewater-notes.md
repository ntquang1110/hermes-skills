## Operating guardrails distilled from the abstracts (for the "lá chắn" row pattern)
1. Competition: K+ outcompetes NH4+ on some zeolites; Na>K>Ca>Mg (26143500, 29194008) — real slurry underperforms clean-lab numbers.
2. Dose scale: effective trial doses are g/L–tens of g/L with 55-min–2-h contact (12 g/L modified zeolite; 60 g/L AD batch; 150 g/L chabazite centrate; 20 g/L bentonite acid AD; 25 g/L montmorillonite) — effects exist at "treatment" dose, not feed-carryover trace dose.
3. Limits: alkaline pH + high dose → Ca2+ release + PO4 precipitation → microbial P deficiency (29194008); background NH4+ >500 mg/L blocks Cu/Zn uptake (30981197); bentonite >2.5% in compost lowers seed GI (22864172).
4. Mineral grains don't dissolve — expect settling with sludge; needs mixing for contact and recovery with sludge (operational inference, flag as such, not a measured number).
