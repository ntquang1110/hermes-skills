## EFSA FEEDAP opinions = best industrial-spec source (reusable pattern)
- EFSA feed-additive opinions are indexed in PubMed + PMC, full text open via PMC OA API, and carry **production process + batch-analysis tables** — ideal for commercial product specs.
- **Chestnut extract** (Diarr-Stop S Plus opinion, EFSA J 2016;14(5):e04472, PMID 42109446, PMC13151647): "extract obtained by aqueous extraction of sweet chestnut (C. sativa)… specified to contain 75.3% hydrolysable tannins, 17% non-tannin substances, 7.1% moisture"; batch analysis 75.6% tannin, ash 1.2%, moisture 6%.
- **Quebracho extract** (EFSA J 2022;20(12):e07699, PMID 36540775, PMC9753461): 5 batches, condensed tannins ~79% by ISO 14088 (hide powder method), protein 0.42%, lipid 0.12%, ash 2.38%; safe max levels in feed: piglets 720 mg/kg, pigs for fattening 860 mg/kg, broilers 400, beef 1580, dairy 1030, sheep/goats 1580, salmonids 1810.
- Retrieval: esearch db=pmc with `"brand/product name"` → resolve real PMID via esearch db=pubmed with title terms → efetch abstract → pmcoa.cgi BioC_xml full text for specs/tables.
