# Electrokinetic Remediation (EKR) — Reference

## Overview

Electrokinetic remediation uses direct current (DC) electric fields (20-100V/m) applied across electrodes buried in contaminated soil to transport heavy metal ions via electromigration and electroosmosis.

## Core Mechanisms

| Mechanism | Direction | Speed | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Electromigration** | Cations → cathode; Anions → anode | 10-60 cm/day/V | Movement of dissolved ions under electric field — primary mechanism for HM removal |
| **Electroosmosis** | Anode → cathode | 20-50 cm/day/V | Flow of pore fluid (water) through charged soil capillaries |
| **Electrophoresis** | Counter-current to electroosmosis | — | Movement of colloids and charged particles |

## Electrode Reactions

| Anode (+) | Cathode (−) |
|---|---|
| 2H₂O → O₂↑ + 4H⁺ + 4e⁻ | 2H₂O + 2e⁻ → H₂↑ + 2OH⁻ |
| pH drops to 2-3 (acid front) | pH rises to 10-12 (base front) |
| Acid dissolves HM from soil surface | OH⁻ precipitates HM as M(OH)₂ |

## Key Parameters

- **Voltage gradient:** 0.5-2.0 V/cm typical
- **Current density:** 1-10 mA/cm²
- **Treatment time:** 7-28 days
- **Pore fluid:** water + electrolyte (citric acid, EDTA, CaCl₂, KH₂PO₄)
- **Electrode material:** graphite (preferred), stainless steel, Ti-coated

## Typical Effectiveness

| Soil type | HM removal | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Kaolinite clay | 85-95% | Best performer — low permeability keeps current focused |
| Clayey sand | 70-85% | |
| Peat/organic | 50-65% | |
| Sandy soil | 40-55% | High permeability → current disperses |

## Critical Problems

### 1. Metal Precipitation at Cathode
OH⁻ generated → pH spike → M²⁺ + 2OH⁻ → M(OH)₂↓ — metal precipitates in soil, cannot be removed.
**Fixes:** Acid injection at cathode, ion-exchange membranes (IEM), polarity reversal every 6-12h.

### 2. Polarization (Voltage Drop)
- **Activation:** Gas bubbles (O₂/H₂) on electrodes → insulate surface. Fix: short polarity reversal.
- **Resistance:** White salt scale (CaCO₃, Mg(OH)₂) builds up. Fix: acid circulation.
- **Concentration:** H⁺ + OH⁻ meet → neutralise → acid front stops advancing. Fix: buffer injection.

### 3. Loss of Beneficial Minerals
Ca²⁺, Mg²⁺, K⁺ are also cations → migrate out with HM. Losses 60-85% typical.
**Fixes:** Inject Ca²⁺/Mg²⁺/K⁺ at cathode to saturate; exchange membranes; post-treatment re-mineralisation (lime + K-Mg fertiliser).
