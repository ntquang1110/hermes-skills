# Peat (Than bùn) Quality Standards — Feed + Fertilizer

## Regulatory references
- QCVN 01-190:2020/BNNPTNT — Feed (thức ăn chăn nuôi) heavy metal limits
- QCVN 01-189:2019/BNNPTNT — Organic fertilizer (phân bón hữu cơ)
- QCVN 03:2023/BTNMT — Soil heavy metal limits
- TCVN 8569:2010 — Humic + fulvic acid determination

## Critical thresholds

| Parameter | Symbol | Unit | Feed limit | Fertilizer limit | Test method |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Arsenic | As | ppm | ≤2 | ≤10 | ICP-MS |
| Lead | Pb | ppm | ≤5 | ≤50 | ICP-MS |
| Mercury | Hg | ppm | ≤0.1 | ≤1 | Hg analyzer |
| Cadmium | Cd | ppm | ≤0.5 | ≤2 | ICP-MS |
| Chromium(VI) | Cr⁶⁺ | ppm | ≤1 | ≤5 | TCVN 7939:2009 |

## Microbiological thresholds

|---|---|---|---|
| Salmonella spp. | KPH /25g | KPH | TCVN 4829:2005 |

## Quality grades

- **A+ (Excellent)**: All parameters pass feed + fertilizer standards. Export-ready.
- **A (Good)**: Metals + microbiology pass, nutrition ≥80%. Ready for domestic use.
- **B (Conditional)**: Metals pass, microbiology slightly high (heat-treat at 70°C). Nutrition ≥60%.
- **C (Fail)**: Metals over limit. Re-treat or discard.

## Testing cost (Vietnam)

- Full metal panel (As, Pb, Hg, Cd, Cr, Cu, Zn, Ni): ~1.5-3M VND
- Full nutrition (pH, OM, HA, FA, N, P, K, Ca, Mg, CEC, EC, Ash): ~2-4M VND
- Full microbiology (7 parameters): ~1.5-3M VND
- **Total full analysis**: ~5-10M VND per sample
