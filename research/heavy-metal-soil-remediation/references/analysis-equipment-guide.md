# Heavy Metal Analysis Equipment Guide

## Why This Matters

After treatment, you need to verify that heavy metal concentrations are below regulatory thresholds (QCVN 03:2023/BTNMT). External lab testing at ~800K/mẫu × 5-7 mẫu/ngày = 4-5.6tr/ngày. Buying your own analytical equipment pays for itself in weeks.

## Equipment Options Comparison

| Option | Investment | Cost/test | Speed | Accuracy | Best for |
|--------|-----------|-----------|-------|----------|----------|
| External lab (ICP-MS/AAS) | 0 | ~800K/mẫu | 1-3 ngày | Highest | Occasional / certification |
| **Hach DR1900 + test kits** ⭐ | **~40-47M VND** | **~$2-4/chỉ tiêu** | **10-30 phút/chỉ tiêu** | **Good (ppm level)** | **Daily QC, on-site** |

## Recommended: Hach DR1900 Portable Spectrophotometer

### Setup cost breakdown
- Máy DR1900: ~$1,200-1,500 (28-35M VND)
- Test kit As (100 tests): ~$200
- Test kit Pb (100 tests): ~$150
- Test kit Cd (100 tests): ~$200
- Test kit Hg (100 tests): ~$250
- **Tổng ~40-47M VND**

### Recurring costs
- Each test kit covers ~100 tests
- Cost per test per element: $1.5-2.5
- Vs external lab: 800K/mẫu → saves 50-100x

### Procedure
1. Sample preparation: acid digestion of soil (HCl/HNO3, ~30-60 min hot plate)
2. Measure on DR1900 with appropriate reagent kit
3. One element at a time (unlike XRF which measures all simultaneously)

### Limits of detection
- As: ~0.01 ppm
- Pb: ~0.05 ppm
- Cd: ~0.01 ppm
- Hg: ~0.001 ppm (with cold vapor technique)

## Alternative: Portable Electrochemical (ASV) Analyzer

Chinese brands from Alibaba/1688:
- Uses Anodic Stripping Voltammetry (ASV)
- Price: $1,500-2,500 (FOB, ~35-60M VND delivered)
- Can detect Hg, As, Cd, Pb after acid extraction
- Requires specific electrodes per element (recurring cost)
- Rủi ro: Không bảo hành tại VN, chất lượng không kiểm chứng, khó mua điện cực thay thế

## Best Option: Used Handheld XRF (if budget permits)

- Olympus Vanta Element / Bruker S1 Titan 600
- New: $4,200-4,800 (~100-115M VND)
- Used: $2,500-4,000 (~60-95M VND)
- Measures all 4 elements simultaneously in 30-60 seconds
- No consumables, no sample prep (just sieve + present to window)
- Non-destructive
