         ↓

   ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

## Detoxification Strategies

| Strategy | Agents | Mechanism | Maturity |
|----------|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Binders** | HMT, bentonite, glucomannan | Physical adsorption | Commercial |
| **Enzymatic** | ZHD (ZEN hydrolase), laccase | Hydrolysis / oxidation | Advanced (An48: 1328× stability) |
| **Microbial** | B. cereus BC7, B. licheniformis CK1 | Biotransformation | Proven in vivo |
| **Antioxidant** | Quercetin, lycopene, Vit C+E + Se | ROS scavenging, Nrf2 activation | Proven in vivo |
| **Combination** | Vit A+C+E + GSH-Se + quercetin | Multi-target | Tested in DON+ZEA co-contamination (2026) |

## Vietnamese Relevance

- **Climate:** Hot-humid tropical → ideal Fusarium growth on imported maize
- **Risk:** >60% of imported maize/corn samples in VN contain detectable ZEA
- **Common co-contamination:** ZEA + DON (same Fusarium species → same crops)
- **Clinical impact:** Vulvar swelling, pseudopregnancy → mistaken for estrus → failed AI
- **Control:** Mycotoxin binders common in VN feed mills; enzymatic solutions emerging 2025-2026
