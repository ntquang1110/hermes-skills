# Output Requirements — ALL Research MUST Cite a Source

## Hard rule
Every research output produced by this skill MUST include:
- **A linked source** (URL, DOI, PMID) — the article/paper being analyzed
- **In-depth analysis** of the source: mechanisms, data, methodology, conclusions
- **Scientific evidence** with specific figures, measurements, statistics

## When no source is found
If search results don't include a retrievable article or paper:
1. Log "No source found for [topic]" 
2. Skip — do NOT produce a research output from unverified/aggregated search snippets alone
3. Move to the next topic in the queue

## Domains affected
This rule applies to ALL domains:
- 🧬 Biology / Immunology / Animal science
- 🧪 Chemistry / Materials
- 🤖 AI / Machine learning  
- 💰 Finance / Economics
- 🔧 Technology / Engineering
- 🔬 Any other research domain

## Rationale
The user explicitly requires: "Tất cả những gì liên quan đến research phải có báo. Phải có chứng minh khoa học đầy đủ." (All research must have an article/paper. Must have full scientific evidence.)

This prevents generating content from:
- News summaries without original sources
- Aggregated search snippets
- General knowledge without citations
- Opinion pieces or marketing content
