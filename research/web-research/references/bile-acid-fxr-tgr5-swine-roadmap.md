## Key Mechanisms at a Glance

1. **FXR feedback loop**: High BA → FXR activation → SHP ↑ → CYP7A1 ↓ → BA synthesis stops
2. **FXR-gut axis**: FXR in ileum → FGF19 → FGFR4 in liver → CYP7A1↓, CYP8B1↓
3. **TGR5 metabolic effects**: BA → TGR5 → cAMP → GLP-1 secretion → insulin ↑, satiety ↑
4. **TGR5 anti-inflammatory**: BA → TGR5 → cAMP/PKA → CREB → competes NF-κB for CBP/p300 → ↓ TNF-α, IL-1β
5. **Microbiota-BA axis**: BSH (Lactobacillus, Bifidobacterium) → deconjugation → 7α-dehydroxylase (Clostridium) → secondary BAs
6. **Porcine uniqueness**: HCA > CDCA in potency for TGR5; HCA activates FXR distinctly from CDCA
