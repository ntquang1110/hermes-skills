## QCVN 01-1:2018/BYT — nước sạch sinh hoạt (TT 41/2018/TT-BYT)

## NIH ODS fact sheets (ods.od.nih.gov — browser required, urllib gets 403)
- **Calcium**: absorbed by active transport (duodenum, vitamin D-dependent) + passive diffusion; ~30% from dairy/fortified foods; RDA 1.000–1.200 mg; supplements usually calcium carbonate or citrate.
- **Magnesium**: cofactor >300 enzyme systems; ~30–40% absorbed; **forms that dissolve well in liquid have higher absorption** (aspartate, citrate, lactate, chloride > oxide, sulfate); RDA 310–420 mg; UL for supplemental Mg 350 mg.
- **Potassium**: ~90% of ingested K absorbed, passive diffusion in small intestine; intracellular ~30× extracellular; Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase gradient → nerve/muscle/kidney function; AI 3.400 mg (men) / 2.600 mg (women), NASEM 2019.
- Sodium-HealthProfessional fact sheet 404s (as of 08/2026) — use WHO 2012 instead.

## Verified PMIDs (use these instead of re-searching)
- Bronner F (1998) *J Nutr* 128(5):917–20 **PMID 9567004** — Ca absorption: active transcellular (duodenum) + passive paracellular; passive transport likely major route for most mineral ions.
- de Baaij JH, Hoenderop JG, Bindels RJ (2015) *Physiol Rev* 95(1):1–46 **PMID 25540137** — Mg²⁺: 2nd most abundant intracellular cation, >600 enzymatic reactions.

- Yoon GL et al. (2003) *Waste Manag* 23(9):825–34 **PMID 14583245** — oyster shell "predominantly composed of calcium carbonate with rare impurities".
- Ahn S, Lee S, Lim S (2025) *Foods* 14(15):2579 **PMID 40807516** — thermally processed oyster shell powder; solubility 0,4–0,7 mg/g; direct low bioavailability → needs fine milling/thermal treatment.
- Meena H et al. (2010) *Int J Ayurveda Res* 1(1):37–40 **PMID 20532096** — shilajit: humus + fulvic acid as "main carrier molecules" for minerals (traditional-medicine evidence, limited).

## Humic substance definitions (IHSS operational definitions, per Wikipedia)
Humic acid (HA): soluble in water at neutral/alkaline pH, insoluble at pH < 2 · Fulvic acid (FA): soluble at **any** pH, smaller molecules · Humin: insoluble at any pH. HA/FA form chelate complexes with metal ions (humic colloids); HS account for 50–90% of soil cation exchange capacity. Extraction: 0.1 M NaOH, HA precipitates at pH 1.

## Positioning guidance for "nguyên liệu kiềm khoáng" B2B docs
- TDS 7.948 mg/L (pH > 12) ≫ QCVN 01-1 TDS limit (1.000 mg/L) → must be framed as a **processing ingredient**, never drinking water; always state dilution required and that finished product must meet QCVN 6-1 (beverage) or QCVN 8-2 (food) heavy-metal limits.
- No medical claims allowed (QCVN 6-1 mục 3.4); present ion roles as nutrition/physiology science, not effects.
