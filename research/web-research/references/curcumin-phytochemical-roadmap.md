## Key takeaways for card building

- **Bioavailability ~1%** is the honest centerpiece — in vitro µM doses are NOT achievable in vivo with plain curcumin; always discuss nanoformulation or piperine (PMID 9619120).
- **Wikipedia Curcumin article is strongly skeptical** (PAINS compound, 29 retracted Aggarwal papers, ~10 liver-injury cases 2004–2022) — do not over-promise; use in card's "Thách thức" section.
- **Sow data is indirect** — the maternal-transfer evidence is a chicken model (41716840); flag as extrapolation needing direct pig studies.
- **ZnO alternative story is the most practice-relevant**: curcumin+prebiotic beat ZnO on FCR while preserving Lactobacillaceae (35298549) — matches the EU ZnO ban context.
