# Porcine piglet diarrhea: natural feed additives (verified evidence bank)

Evidence bank compiled 2026-08-07 via PubMed E-utilities (esearch + efetch). All PMIDs verified from abstracts. Use for feed-additive / chăn nuôi research — natural, no-antibiotic angle.

## Disease mechanism
- PWD (post-weaning diarrhea) = fluid/electrolyte absorption-secretion imbalance in the gut; ETEC F4/K88 enterotoxins drive Cl- secretion > absorption → diarrhea. PMID 38800731 (Tang, Anim Nutr 2024).
- Feed factors worsening PWD: high crude protein, antigenic proteins, high acid-binding capacity, DON contamination → lowering dietary CP is a cornerstone.

## Group 1: Tannins (chestnut + quebracho) — strongest recent evidence
- Mechanism: direct anti-ETEC, reduced fecal shedding, astringent mucosa, antioxidant.
- PMID 32106264 (Girard 2020 PLoS One): 2% chestnut extract ↓ fecal score, ↓ % diarrhea, ↓ ETEC shedding (P<0.001) in ETEC F4ac-challenged piglets.
- PMID 40061905 (Ollagnier 2025 Front Vet Sci): 7.5 g/kg tannin extract ↓ fecal score + ↓ antimicrobial resistance genes; tannin + potentiated ZnO (150 mg Zn/kg) reduced antibiotic need.
- PMID 39852918 (Sall 2025 Vet Sci): farm trial, 254 piglets, oral tannin complementary feed ↓ diarrhea antibiotic use by 84% (P=0.001) vs paromomycin control.
- PMID 34204108 (Dell'Anno 2021 Animals): 0.75% tannin + 0.25% leonardite + 0.2% tributyrin, 28 d, ↓ diarrhea, ↑ lactobacilli:coliform (P<0.01).
- Dosage: 0.5-2% feed (5-20 g/kg); best evidenced at 7.5 g/kg.
- Stability: BEST of all groups — heat-stable through pelleting, no evaporation.
- Side effects: high dose ↓ protein digestibility (anti-nutritional), ↓ intake. Safe at 0.5-1%.

## Group 2: Saccharomyces boulardii (live yeast probiotic) — safest
- Mechanism: dose-dependent blocking of ETEC adhesion to enterocytes, ↓ pro-inflammatory gene expression, ↑ Bacteroidiaceae ↓ Prevotellaceae, stabilizes weaning dysbiosis. PMID 34572682 (Gresse 2021 Antibiotics).
- PMID 37576772 (Parada 2023 Vet World): 1×10^12 CFU/ton feed substituted prophylactic antibiotics (tiamulin, amoxicillin), ↓ diarrhea, ↑ weight gain.
- PMID 26159939 (Hancox 2015 Animal): single oral dose 3.3×10^9 CFU to neonatal piglets ↓ diarrhea days (P<0.01), firmer feces — works day 1 of life, no need to wait for feed.
- PMID 36630002 (Dang 2024 Probiotics Antimicrob Proteins): probiotic + paraprobiotic + hydrolyzed yeast mix ≈ 3000 ppm ZnO for growth + PWD control.
- Dosage: 10^9-10^12 CFU/kg feed.
- Stability: live yeast dies in pelleting >70°C. Use paraprobiotic/heat-killed; heat-killed L. acidophilus outperformed live in one trial. PMID 39272313 (Miao 2024 Animals).
- Side effects: essentially none.

## Group 3: Essential oils (thymol, carvacrol, cinnamaldehyde, eugenol)
- Mechanism: membrane disruption antibacterial, ↓ TNF-α/IL-6, ↑ IgA/G/M, ↑ jejunum/ileum villus height.
- PMID 38359649 (Hernández-García 2024 Res Vet Sci): meta-analysis 31 trials — ↓ diarrhea (P<0.001), ↑ ADG, ↓ FCR.
- PMID 35760400 (Grando 2023 Anim Biosci): EO blend 200-400 mg/kg ↓ diarrhea, better liver SOD.
- Dosage: 100-400 mg/kg feed.
- Stability: WORST — evaporates in pelleting; must be microencapsulated or post-pellet.
- Side effects: strong smell ↓ intake, mucosal irritation at high dose.

## Group 4: Organic acids / butyrate
- Mechanism: gastric pH ↓ inhibits pathogens; butyrate upregulates tight junctions (ZO-1, claudin), ↓ inflammation, feeds Roseburia/F. prausnitzii. PMID 39795009 (Connolly 2024 Animals); PMID 33670980 (Bonetti 2021 Animals, "Towards Zero Zinc Oxide").
- Stability: use salt forms (coated Na-butyrate, Ca-formate); free acids hygroscopic, corrosive to equipment.

## Group 5: Plant polyphenols / polysaccharides (support)
- PMID 40923241 (Cho 2025 J Anim Sci): green tea extract + butyric acid + vit K — no diarrhea, ↑ IgA/IgG, ↓ BSH bacteria.
- PMID 34679793 (Rajković 2021 Animals): grape extract ATTD comparable/better than therapeutic amoxicillin.
- PMID 42558313 (Liu 2026 Front Vet Sci): mulberry leaf flavonoids review — ↓ diarrhea in weaned piglets.
- PMID 39663725 (Huang 2024 J Agric Food Chem): plant polysaccharides (inulin, β-glucan, pectin) ↓ diarrhea via microbiota modulation.

## Practical verdict (farm-level)
1. Choose 1-2 actives, not a pile. Safest + most feed-stable combo: chestnut tannin 0.5-1% + S. boulardii 10^10-10^11 CFU/kg (tannin kills ETEC in lumen, yeast protects epithelium).
2. Neonatal vs post-weaning diarrhea differ. Boulardii works day 1 via direct oral dose (PMID 26159939).
3. Pelleting 95°C/120s kills live biological actives (PMID 34208387 phage encapsulation reference) — post-pellet or protected forms.
4. Lower crude protein in feed = less substrate for ETEC (PMID 38800731, 39795009).
5. Severe diarrhea: rehydrate/electrolytes FIRST — dehydration kills; natural actives are long-term.
6. No natural active is "absolutely safe" — respect dose ranges, test one pen before herd-wide use.
