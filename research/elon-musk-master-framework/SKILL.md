# Elon Musk Master Framework

## Tong quan

Tai lieu tong hop day du ve tu duy, he thong van hanh va chien luoc san pham cua Elon Musk. Duoc thiet ke cho founder da cong ty (Kiem Saphia, TFO, meTelligent) co the ap dung ngay.

## Cau truc tai lieu

### Phan 1: Tu Duy & Mental Models
- First Principles Thinking (dinh nghia, vi du SpaceX/Tesla, bang ap dung cho TACN/ERP/voice AI)
- Tu duy xac suat (expected value, Bayesian update, survivorship filter)
- Inversion (dao nguoc van de)
- Second-Order Effects (Tesla mo patent, tai su dung ten lua)
- Feedback Loops & Musk's Algorithm (5 buoc: Hoi -> Xoa -> Don gian -> Tang toc -> Tu dong hoa)
- Mental Masturbation Filter (4 hinh thuc lang phi)

### Phan 2: He Thong Van Hanh (8 muc)
1. The Algorithm 5 buoc + checklist ap dung
2. Cach dieu hanh hop (6 quy tac, vi du SpaceX/Tesla/X)
3. Triet ly tuyen dung (nang luc > kinh nghiem, 3 cau hoi tu)
4. Dat muc tieu First Principles (deadline bat kha thi co chu y)
5. Quan tri khung hoang (mau 5 buoc + playbook)
6. Phong cach giao tiep (email <5 cau, mass-email, carrot & stick)
7. Quan ly thoi gian 6 cong ty (time blocking 5 phut, "cong ty cua tuan")
8. Delete Step (xoa truoc toi uu, "Delete Review" hang quy)

### Phan 3: Chien Luoc San Pham & Doi Moi
- Bo loc "van de quan trong" (5 linh vuc, tieu chi chon du an)
- Tich hop doc (vertical integration — quy tac mua vs tu lam)
- Triet ly vong lap (test-to-failure, Starship no la du lieu)
- Quan tri rui ro (muc lieu vs bien phap phong ngu)
- To chuc phang + van hoa hardcore
- Diem mu & nghich ly (FSD, Neuralink, coi thuong doi thu TQ)

### Phan 4: Tong Ket & Lo Trinh 90 Ngay
- Bo cong cu hang ngay (8 framework + cau hoi khoi dong)
- Lo trinh 3 thang (Thang 1: Xoa, Thang 2: Toc do, Thang 3: Khung hoang)
- Ap dung cho tung cong ty (Kiem Saphia, TFO, meTelligent)
- 7 cau noi de ghi nho hang ngay
- Triet ly cot loi: Toc do > Hoan hao. Xoa > Toi uu. Hien truong > Phong hop. Bang chung > Bang cap.
